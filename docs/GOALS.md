# Personal Desk: goals

## Audience and experience

Visitors should learn who Leonardo is through a tactile desk they can explore. The desk should feel personal, warm, and calm. It is a top-down oak surface holding an ivory introduction note, three polaroids, and a stamped travel postcard. Paper texture, subtle rotation, and soft shadows follow the approved visual concept.

## First version

- Visitors can move objects, open details, and use Back and Forward while their arrangement stays put.
- Each detail has a shareable URL and works on a direct load.
- Desktop and mobile have separate arrangements saved in the visitor's browser, with a visible reset control.
- Phones use a scrollable desk and dedicated drag handles.
- The site works with keyboard navigation, visible focus, image alternatives, and reduced motion.
- Sample biography, photos, and travel content are identified as placeholders.

## Acceptance

1. Moving at least two objects, opening a detail, then going Back leaves both objects where they were.
2. Refreshing a detail URL keeps the saved arrangement visible behind the detail.
3. Objects remain reachable after resizing, rotating a phone, and resetting the desk.
4. A visitor can open and close every detail without a pointer. Escape closes details and returns focus to the opened object.
5. The desktop and mobile layouts remain readable without clipped primary content or accidental horizontal scrolling.

The first version needs no account, backend, analytics, or CMS. The rubber duck uses Three.js, viewed directly from above.
